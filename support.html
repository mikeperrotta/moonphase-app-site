Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app request location access?

Location is used to calculate accurate moonrise and moonset times for your specific geographic position.

Is my location stored?

No. Location coordinates are transmitted only to retrieve astronomical data and are not stored on external servers.

How do I disable location access?

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → MoonPhase, and adjust permissions as desired.
