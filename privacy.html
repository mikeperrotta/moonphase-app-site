Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Overview

MoonPhase is designed to provide astronomical information without collecting, selling, or tracking personal data.

Information We Collect

If you grant permission, the app accesses your device’s approximate location (latitude and longitude).

How We Use Information

Location coordinates are used solely to calculate moonrise and moonset times.

These calculations are performed entirely on your device. Your latitude and longitude are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or analytics providers. Location coordinates never leave your device.

Data Storage

The app does not store personal data on external servers. Any data the app generates is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

MoonPhase does not send data to any third-party service. Astronomical values are calculated on your device, and the moon imagery included in the app is bundled with it — courtesy of the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio.

Your Choices

You may deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device’s settings. Deleting the app removes any locally stored data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: moonphase.app.contact@gmail.com
